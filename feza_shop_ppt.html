Review the Technology

100% Student-Made Technology

FEZA Shop is built and maintained entirely by the Programming Club, utilizing core web technologies for a robust, secure, and user-friendly experience.

HTML (Structure)

Defines the entire structure and content: from the navigation bar and product layout to the hero section, cart modal, and footer.

CSS (Style)

Defines the website's visual presentation, styling HTML elements by setting colors, fonts, layout, and responsiveness for a cohesive UI.

JavaScript (Function)

Manages dynamic functionality and state, specifically used here to **save and retrieve the user's shopping cart** data securely.

Our Strategic Goals: Why We Built Feza Shop

1. Streamline the Supply Chain

We eliminate the hassle of physical trips and confusing supply lists by **centralizing all approved school and club gear online**. Shop quickly and confidently, any time.

2. Ensure Transparency & Value

Our site provides **clear, upfront pricing** and secure digital payment options. This transparency, combined with occasional online-only deals, ensures you receive the best value.

3. Provide Accessible Convenience

The website is built around your busy schedule, allowing you to order in three minutes, giving you **peace of mind** that your child will have the exact, approved items they need.
