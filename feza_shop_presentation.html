The Bottlenecks

Current challenges in the school supply ecosystem.

Manual Friction

Buying uniforms once meant physical visits during limited hours. Transactions were manual, and verification was slow.

Opaque Tracking

Once an order was placed, parents had zero visibility into delivery status, leading to "Where is my order?" calls.

Security Gaps

Managing school funds for clubs and supplies via loose cash or untracked digital transfers posed audit risks.

The Ecosystem Solution

Centralized Security PINs

Automated email verification for wallet top-ups using a secure FastAPI background service.

Live Delivery Tracking

Real-time GPS tracking with Leaflet.js and a premium glassmorphism mobile interface.

Multi-Gateway Payments

Native support for M-Pesa, Flutterwave, and Stripe integrated into a unified checkout.
